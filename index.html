Entry-Level BA Job-Ready Syllabus

No academic fluff or endless theory. Just the core skills and interview frameworks you need to pass business analyst rounds and start contributing on day one.

  • Requirements Elicitation & Stakeholder Interviewing 1. Requirements Elicitation & Stakeholder Interviewing

    Why it matters: Tests behavioral questions on turning vague user demands into exact problem statements.

  • 2. Business Requirements Documentation (BRD vs. SRS & User Stories)

    Why it matters: Documentation is your main output; interviewers often make you write user stories on the spot.

  • 3. Business Process Mapping (AS-IS vs. TO-BE)

    Why it matters: Technical interview rounds use process mapping whiteboarding to test your workflow logic.

  • 4. Agile & Scrum Framework Essentials

    Why it matters: Shows you understand backlogs, sprint cycles, Jira tickets, and won't slow down engineering teams.

  • 5. Practical Data Analysis & SQL for BAs

    Why it matters: Validates that you can run your own data queries rather than relying on engineers for simple numbers.

  • 6. Gap Analysis & Root Cause Frameworks

    Why it matters: Crucial for cracking business case study interviews using structured frameworks like the 5 Whys.

  • 7. Wireframing & Prototyping Basics

    Why it matters: Demonstrates end-to-end product thinking by letting you visually pitch user flows before development.

  • 8. Interview Prep & Application Tactics

    Why it matters: Resume positioning, behavioral answers, handling case studies, and getting past ATS filters.

  • 9. Deep Academic Rabbit Holes (For Nerds)

    Why it matters: Deep theory on BABOK, systems thinking, formal BPMN 2.0 notation, and enterprise architecture models.
